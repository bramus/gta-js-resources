San Andreas 2 Tank 60, 189, 3 This tank is inside an impound, to open it you have to drive right down to the very bottom-left corner of the map and pick up the superbike there (it's green and it's hidden in the grass so it's a bit difficult to spot) you have to jump onto the little island (which is tricky) and pick up the 'HAYULP!', it should say 'THE DOORS TO DESTRUCTION ARE OPEN!'. You then have to jump back and go to the tank, the gate of the impound (on the eastern fence) should now be open. See Tankmap. Colour: Light Blue.
